PutPlay

put.io for webOS — original files, embedded subtitles, your remote and other goodies. Unofficial, and built for TVs that time and put.io's own web app left behind.

What it does

  • Plays your files as uploaded (MKV/MP4) — with put.io MP4 and HLS as automatic fallbacks when the original misbehaves
  • Subtitles: MKV-embedded first, then your put.io subtitles (ASS/SSA and SRT) — the search asks for your account's languages plus the one you set in the app
  • Audio track switching — and when the TV's player refuses, it tells you the truth instead of pretending it worked
  • Reads your .cbz comics — only the pages you look at are streamed, in sheets of one or two pages, left-to-right or right-to-left, and the book reopens where you left it
  • Opens archives (.zip/.cbz) without downloading them, and views pictures with fit/2×/4× zoom and panning
  • Music files play under a now-playing strip while you keep browsing, reading, and watching — it collapses to a slim corner row and back
  • Resumes where you left off; remembers your source and language preferences
  • Docked video — Back from the full player docks it to a corner: it keeps playing while you browse or read; restore, mini, or stop from the dock
  • When a video ends, the next one in the same folder starts automatically (on by default, off in Settings)
  • Skip intro, recap and credits — segment times from TheIntroDB, AniSkip for anime, and the introdb.app consensus, matched to your file automatically (episodes by marker, marker-less anime names, movies by title); auto-skip or ask
  • An info tab on the player — the episode/film's still, synopsis, rating, genres, and date, from TMDb (anime from AniList)
  • Playback speed 0.5×–2×

Uses your own put.io account against the public put.io API — files stay on put.io. Not affiliated with put.io or LG.
