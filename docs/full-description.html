PutPlay

put.io for webOS — original files, embedded subtitles, your remote and other goodies. Unofficial, and built for TVs that time and put.io's own web app left behind.

What it does

  • Plays your files as uploaded (MKV/MP4) — with put.io MP4 and HLS as automatic fallbacks when the original misbehaves
  • Subtitles: MKV-embedded first, then your put.io subtitles (ASS/SSA and SRT)
  • Audio track switching — and when the TV's player refuses, it tells you the truth instead of pretending it worked
  • Resumes where you left off; remembers your source and language preferences

Uses your own put.io account against the public put.io API — files stay on put.io. Not affiliated with put.io or LG.
